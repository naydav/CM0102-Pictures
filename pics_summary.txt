[size=150][b]Naydav's CM0102 1280x800 Background Image Pack[/b][/size]

A set of background images for CM0102 at native resolution (RGN 1280x800): clubs, nations, national competitions, and club competitions.

Originally created for personal use — now shared with the community.

[size=120][b]What's included[/b][/size]

One of the most complete and up-to-date CM0102 image collections available.
A consistent visual style across clubs, nations, and competitions, with [color=red]references to iconic moments in football history[/color].
Each background was selected to preserve the [color=red]authentic football atmosphere[/color] and make every screen feel alive.
